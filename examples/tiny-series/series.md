# Fake Heiress Reboot

## Premise
A delivery rider discovers she is the missing heiress of a collapsing tech family, but she keeps her old identity to expose who erased her.

## Target Audience / Tone
Fast, emotional, modern short drama; revenge, identity reversal, sharp cliffhangers.

## Series Engine
Each episode reveals one lie about the family while the heroine weaponizes her underestimated public identity.

## Episode Arc Overview
Episode 1: public humiliation becomes the first reversal.

## Visual Direction
Neon corporate interiors, rainy streets, high-contrast closeups, strong heroine silhouette.
