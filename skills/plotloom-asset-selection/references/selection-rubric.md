# Selection Rubric

## Character Consistency
Does the candidate preserve face, age, outfit, body type, and visual identity from references?

## Visual Continuity
Does motion, lighting, style, and environment remain coherent with surrounding clips?

## Short-Drama Clarity
Can the viewer understand the conflict and emotional beat quickly?

## Hook Strength
Does the first or final moment make the next clip/episode feel necessary?

## Model Artifacts
Check hands, faces, text, warped props, temporal flicker, impossible motion, and incoherent backgrounds.

## Reroll vs Prompt Revision
- Reroll same prompt when intent is right but artifact quality is poor.
- Revise prompt when the candidate consistently misses character, action, camera, or ending frame.

## Selected Semantics
Accepted files are copied to `selected.*`; previous selected files are backed up as `selected-prev-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.*`; candidates remain untouched.

## Video Review
Review one candidate at a time and avoid silently accepting ambiguous candidates.
